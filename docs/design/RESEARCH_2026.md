# RESEARCH 2026 — why the screens don't feel right

Competitor and platform research read against an audit of what THRØ's iPhone app and website
actually do today. Internal. PD references are fine here; they never reach a player.

**Where the counts come from.** Every number below was taken on **18 September 2026** and re-checked at commit
**`fe2745f`** (`git rev-parse --short HEAD`), and the command that produces it is named beside it, so a reader
re-derives a count rather than trusting it. Several working-tree files were mid-change while the counts were
taken — four uncommitted API and app test files, none of which any count here reads — and `apps/web/thro.css`
changed earlier the same day (PD-135 added a `.skeleton` block), so its counts are of the file as it now stands
and say so. A count over a file still being edited is a count with a shelf life; re-run the command, don't cite this.

## The answer

The style guide is kept. The vocabulary is not.

THRØ has one generated token layer and the app mostly obeys it — 249 uses of `spacing2` — but 47 raw
`.padding()` numbers survive in `packages/client-ios/Sources`, 37 outside the ThroDesign layer that
owns spacing. What is missing is one word for each job. There are **six ways to top a page**
(`TopBar`, `BoardHeader`, `Masthead`, You's inline header, `PageBar`, `MatchHeader`), **four card
vocabularies** with two paddings and two stroke styles, and **three row components** at two heights.
A player going Discover → Event → back → Leagues meets three different tops in three taps, so the app
reads as several apps that share a palette. Three axes have no scale at all: icon size (eleven raw
values), border width (`lineWidth: 1` written twenty times against eight uses of the token), and
badge size.

**The chrome breaks at large text.** `BottomBar` (`ThroDesign/Navigation.swift:116`) is a hand-built
`HStack(spacing: 0)` with five tabs and no `dynamicTypeSize` cap in the file or at its one mount
(`ThroRootView.swift:636`). At AX5 the labels wrap to "Ho/me", "Pla/y", "Dis/cov/er", the bar grows to
roughly 500pt, "PROFILE" is clipped off the You header and "Your teams" leaves the screen entirely: a
player at that size cannot see their own team. Apple's `TabView` degrades to a list at those sizes; a
hand-built bar inherits none of that. `Icon` is a fixed frame too, so a doubled name sits beside a
chevron that has not moved and the rows stop looking drawn.

**Production is empty, and this document must be read that way.** `GET https://api.thro.uk/v1/leagues`
returns 329 leagues, every one standing `listed`, zero run here, three carrying any season;
`https://api.thro.uk/v1/events` returns `{"events":[]}`. Live, Discover, fixtures, standings and tables
are critiqued below as though they held data. In production nearly every one is an empty state. The
empty state is not an edge case in THRØ; it is the product as shipped.

Hierarchy is the loudest fault: Home shows two full-width primaries, Discover three, the signed-out
Account four ways in. And the web is not on the system — 184 raw lengths, no type scale, no face.

## The ten changes

Ranked by felt impact per unit of work; number 1 first. Each names only what the table does not — the file to open, the cost, the way it goes wrong.

### 1. Make the bottom bar survive large text
Apply a `dynamicTypeSize` ceiling to `BottomBar` (`ThroDesign/Navigation.swift:116`) as the keypad
already does, let the items stack, and drop to four tabs while there — Hoober's dock limit and
Apple's. **Hours** for the cap, a day with the merge; low risk, but walk every tab at AX5, because
this is the one change whose bug is invisible at default sizes.

### 2. One primary action per screen
One filled green button per screen, everything else secondary, ghost or a row —
`ThroRootView.swift:724`, `DiscoverScreen.swift:15`, `AccountScreens.swift:13`. **One day**, almost
all deletion. Someone loses a shortcut: keep every route reachable, just quieter.

### 3. Design for the empty state, because it is the product
Board-shaped `ThroNothingYet` when the emptiness is the page, `EmptyState` card when it is a section, the
four named states, an error colour on the web, and "Start a league on the desk" demoted so it stops being
the loudest thing on every league page. Stripe's under-14-word rule applies to these bodies and only these.
**One day.** The risk is leaving it: the most-seen screen in production is the least designed.

### 4. One pinned-action component
`ThroBottomAction` (`ThroDesign/Slate.swift:216`) on every screen that commits. Record result
hand-rolls it with no hairline (`LeagueScreens.swift:1283`), so the button floats over scrolled
content; with a rule above it the screen has a floor. **Hours**, near-zero risk.

### 5. Put the web on the design system
Extend `check_tokens_exist.py` to `apps/web/thro.css`; replace its 25 raw font-sizes with type tokens;
give `body` and `h1` a size; ship Archivo with an `@font-face`. **One day.** Subset the face to Latin
and the weights used, or the payload is the regression.

### 6. One header, two faces
Collapse the six tops into one `ThroHeader` with a `paper` face and a `board` face — `Navigation.swift:8`,
`AccountDesign.swift:27`, `ThroRootView.swift:952` and `:1682`, `ClubScreens.swift:83`, `Scoring.swift:206`.
Delete the 20 unreferenced spacing tokens in the same pass and collapse the four names that resolve to 20.
**One to two days.** Every top changes at once — walk the simulator, and check Android first: one generator feeds both.

### 7. One row, one card
Fold `LinkRow` (`AccountScreens.swift:167`) and `SettingsRow` (`ThroRootView.swift:1785`) into
`CardRow` (`AccountDesign.swift:129`) at one height — 52 against 56 today — with one padding, one
surface, the slots, and `.strokeBorder` everywhere (`Readiness.swift:764` is the one `.stroke`).
**One day.** Screenshot the account area before and after.

### 8. Make the chrome grow with the text
`Icon` takes a scaled frame (`ThroDesign/Icon.swift:119`); the badge in `OrganisationRow` stops being
a literal 48 (`ThroDesign/Club.swift:128`); `IconTile.side` (`AccountDesign.swift:109`) and
`ThroChoiceRow.labelWidth = 108` (`ThroDesign/Slate.swift:243`) become intrinsic. **One to two days**
plus a large-type pass. The match-setup no-scroll test on an SE may fail; that is the test working.

### 9. Halve the sentences
A test over the string catalogue: no sentence over 25 words, the tighter under-14-word rule on
empty-state bodies only. Worst first: `SettingsScreen.swift:290` (44 words), `Readiness.swift:651`
(39), `LeagueScreens.swift:472` (37), `apps/web/terms.html` (55). Say the local-storage promise once,
in one wording. **Half a day** plus the test. The voice is the founder's: cut clauses, do not rewrite.

### 10. Open lists where the reader is
Team fixtures anchors on the next unplayed row (`TeamFixtureScreens.swift:14`); Live gets a week pager
instead of seven stacked blocks (`ThroRootView.swift:1824`). No citation is offered: the FotMob
payload field the earlier draft quoted twice does not exist at that URL, and the argument does not
need it. **Two days.** The anchor is wrong at a season's end; fall back to the last result.

## What we are not taking

- **Vercel's aesthetic reject list.** Take its numeric rules — tabular figures for anything compared,
  numeric columns right-aligned, animate only transform and opacity ([guidelines](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines/main/AGENTS.md)). Its ban on cards, badges and any colour that is not data would delete the product.
- **Liquid Glass.** Chalk on translucency over a green field is the exact failure case, and Things 3
  spending a release cycle on it is the warning, not the example.
- **Streaks and guilt.** The loop works and breaking it predicts leaving ([The Decision Lab](https://thedecisionlab.com/insights/consumer-insights/streak-creep-the-perils-of-too-much-gamification) — analysis, not a controlled study). Darts is weekly: nudge a fixture, never a lapsed day.
- **GOV.UK's one-thing-per-page.** MoJ published that applying it literally made their own tool slow
  for repeat users ([Design in government](https://designnotes.blog.gov.uk/2022/03/21/going-beyond-the-gov-uk-design-system-for-moj-forms-professional-users/)). Scoring is repeat use. Edit in place.
- **A stats screen built from charts.** Stripe sizes one chart at 180px inside a card, plus a headline
  and a title ([chart layout](https://docs.stripe.com/stripe-apps/patterns/chart-layout.md)); stack three and the no-scrolling rule is gone. One chart, answer above it.
- **Camera scoring and hardware.** Dartsmind and OMNI remove entry; neither has a season, a standing
  or a captain. Do not replace the keypad while the league seam is open.
