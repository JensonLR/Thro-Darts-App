Delete your account

How

In the app: You → your profile → Delete my account. It asks once more, naming what is about to happen, and then it is done. You do not need this page. It exists so the way can be found without the app in your hand.

What goes

  • Your name, and your age band.
  • Every way you sign in: Apple, Google and any passkey.
  • Every session, on this phone and any other, at once.
  • Your friends, and any friend code you have given out.
  • Your claim on your player record, so nothing about you can be shown again.

What stays, and why

  • Matches you have played. A leg is the other player’s record too, and a league’s table stands on it. The result stays, with no name on it and nothing pointing back to you.
  • Matches scored on your phone. They were never sent anywhere unless you sent them; delete them from Home if you want them gone as well.

This cannot be undone. Signing in again with the same Apple ID makes a brand new account with nothing in it.

If you cannot use the app

Lost the phone you signed in on, or the app will not open? There is no way to ask us yet: THRØ has no contact address published. Until there is, the account can only be deleted from the app. We will ask you to prove you can sign in, because otherwise anyone could ask for anyone else’s account to be deleted.

THRØ has no contact address yet. Nothing on this page asks you to write to one.
